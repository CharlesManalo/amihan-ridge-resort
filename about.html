Atok · Highland eco resort

A place with a point of view.

A modest retreat concept where the landscape stays at the center, and the visitor learns to move through it with care.

Background

Rooted in the landscape.

Amihan Ridge imagines a small-scale eco resort in the highlands of Atok, Benguet. The concept pairs cabin and tent stays with guided walks, garden visits, and spaces for small retreat groups.

Target market

Who we welcome.

Nature seekers, couples, small groups, and retreat teams who value quiet stays and guided outdoor experiences.

Mission

What guides us.

To offer restorative outdoor stays while encouraging careful use of water, respectful local engagement, and responsible trail behavior.

Vision

Where we hope to go.

To show how a small tourism concept can support local livelihoods and help guests leave a lighter footprint.

History & development

From idea to invitation.

A fictional concept timeline created for this academic project.

2024

Listening to the landscape

The concept started with low-impact stays and a focus on small groups rather than large crowds.

2025

Following the trail

Cabins, garden visits, guided walks, and a retreat pavilion were planned as complementary experiences.

2026

A place to pause

The academic resort concept now includes clear trail expectations, sample itineraries, and a pace-based trip planner.

Tourism significance

Connected to the place.

The concept encourages guided exploration, local purchasing, and longer stays that give travelers time to understand highland landscapes without rushing through them.

Location

Atok, Benguet.

Our eco commitments

Care is part of the stay.

Carry less waste.

Refill bottles, sort waste, and take every wrapper back from a walk. Leave plants, flowers, and rocks where they belong.

Keep it local.

The concept favors local guides and seasonal ingredients. Respect farm work and ask before entering private land.

Move with care.

Stay on designated trails, use water thoughtfully, and let guides decide when conditions call for a different plan.

Proposed practices for this fictional concept; no environmental certification is claimed.

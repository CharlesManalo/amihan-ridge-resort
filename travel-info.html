Transportation options

From Baguio City

Pre-arranged car or locally confirmed highland transport

Baguio through La Trinidad and Tublay on the Baguio–Bontoc/Halsema road corridor to Sayangan, Atok.

Allow 1.5–2.5 hours

Check current road and tourism advisories. Rain, fog or road work can greatly increase travel time.

From Metro Manila

Baguio-bound bus, then a separately arranged Atok road transfer

Take a confirmed Baguio service from Metro Manila, then continue north through La Trinidad and Halsema to Atok.

Allow 7–10 hours of travel, plus connection waits

The onward leg is separately arranged; no direct resort shuttle or direct Manila–Atok service is asserted.

Tourist Guide

Arrive informed. Travel with care.

Best time to visit

December–February is a useful planning window. A cool-season planning window; bring warm layers and allow for changing mountain conditions. This is seasonal guidance, not a weather guarantee.

Weather considerations

PAGASA describes a broad dry season from December to May and rainy season from June to November. December to February is cooler; March to May is hotter. Local rainfall varies. Check current PAGASA forecasts, tropical cyclone bulletins and marine warnings before outdoor trips.

What to bring

Carry warm layers, a waterproof shell, closed shoes with grip, a refillable bottle, a torch, a small day pack, and personal medication.

Dress recommendations

Dress in layers and use closed footwear on trails. Bring dry socks and a rain shell even when the morning looks clear.

Safety reminders

Travel mountain roads in daylight when practical, check road advisories, follow your guide, and turn back if conditions deteriorate. Discuss fitness needs before any trek.

Local customs

Respect private farms and local traditions. Ask permission before photographing residents or entering cultivated land; support local guides and producers.

Environmental guidelines

Use designated trails, pack out waste, refill bottles, and leave plants untouched. Use water thoughtfully and respect quiet hours and fire restrictions.

Accessibility

Highland paths and tent areas can be steep and uneven. The concept offers easier garden activities, but step-free rooms and bathroom access must be confirmed; no universal-access claim is made.

Estimated budget

Allow ₱2,800–₱9,500 per stay unit per night plus ₱300–₱600 per person for selected activities and roughly ₱600–₱1,200 daily for meals. Transport and specialist gear are extra; figures are estimates.

Tourist etiquette

Let guides set the pace, keep trail noise low, avoid picking flowers, and leave farm gates and growing areas as you found them.

Emergency information

Dial 911

For life-threatening emergencies in the Philippines, call Unified 911. It coordinates police, fire, medical and rescue response. Tell the dispatcher your actual location and follow their instructions. Keep essential medication and an emergency contact accessible.
